Questions

Good to know

Is it free?

The customer and tiffin-provider tools are free for everyone, and so is a basic marketplace listing. Providers who want extra visibility can pay for an ad in the marketplace.

How does advertising work?

An ad gives you a “Sponsored” spot at the top of the results, but only for customers within the radius of the plan you choose, around your kitchen. People far away never see it, and you are not charged per click.

Do I need to be good with phones?

No. Each screen does one thing, buttons are big, and the words are plain. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use this.

How does a customer get a login?

Add the customer with their phone number. They then create a password using that same number. Only numbers a provider has added can sign up.

How long do I stay logged in?

30 days on the same phone. Log out any time from Settings.

Do I have to use Telegram?

No, it’s optional. Without it you still see everything in the app. With it, you get alerts the moment a customer takes leave. Here is how to set it up.

What if I forget my password?

Providers: tap “Forgot password?” on the login page and we send a 6-digit code to your connected Telegram. Customers: ask your tiffin provider for a one-time reset link. Anyone can also contact support. Step-by-step help.
